Pallas Athena

Pallas Athena represents strategic synthesis. When I read a technical manual, I look for ideas that recur and dependencies the manual leaves implicit. Those relationships can show where a learner needs an explanation before attempting a task.

Mercury

Mercury represents language and the movement of information between domains. In technical translation, that can mean finding an explanation a new reader can follow while preserving the distinctions that matter in the source.

Learning Patterns

A manual might organise its chapters by component. A learner may need to start with a procedure, or with the concepts that explain why its steps appear in that order. The question I ask is what someone needs to understand before the next step makes sense.

Pixel to Teach

Pixel to Teach explores how to turn technical documentation into structured learning material, including glossaries, modules and questions, with links back to the source.
